# Nimbus Works Employee Handbook

This handbook is fictional. It exists so the HR helpdesk agent in this series has real-looking policy text to search.

## 1. Leave policy

### 1.1 Earned leave
Every full-time employee earns 1.5 days of earned leave for each completed month of service, which is 18 days a year. Earned leave must be applied for at least 3 working days in advance, except in an emergency. Up to 30 days of unused earned leave carry over to the next calendar year. Anything above 30 days lapses on 31 December.

### 1.2 Sick leave
Employees get 8 days of sick leave a year. Sick leave does not carry over. For more than 2 consecutive days of sick leave, upload a doctor's certificate within 7 days of returning to work.

### 1.3 Casual leave
Employees get 4 days of casual leave a year, for short personal needs. Casual leave can be taken for at most 2 consecutive days and cannot be combined with earned leave.

### 1.4 Leave approval
All leave is approved by the employee's direct manager. If the manager does not respond within 2 working days, the request goes to the manager's manager. Leave that overlaps a public holiday counts only working days.

## 2. Work from home

Employees may work from home up to 2 days a week after completing probation. Work-from-home days need manager approval in the HR portal by 6 pm on the previous working day. During the first 90 days of employment, work from home needs written approval from the department head.

## 3. Holidays

The company observes 10 fixed public holidays and 2 floating holidays a year. Employees choose their floating holidays in January from the published list. The full holiday calendar is available in the HR portal.

## 4. Payroll

### 4.1 Pay day
Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month. If the last day is a holiday, salaries are credited on the previous working day.

### 4.2 Deductions
Monthly deductions include provident fund (12 percent of basic pay), professional tax as per state rules, and income tax deducted at source based on the declared tax regime. Employees can change their tax regime declaration once a year, in April.

### 4.3 Payslip questions
Payslips are published in the HR portal by the 3rd of the following month. For a mismatch in a payslip, raise a payroll ticket within 30 days of the payslip date.

## 5. Expenses

Business travel and client meals are reimbursed against original receipts. Claims must be submitted within 45 days of the expense. Claims above INR 25,000 need approval from the department head before they are paid. Personal expenses, fines and alcohol are never reimbursed.

## 6. Notice period and exit

The notice period is 60 days for employees and 90 days for managers. Earned leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period. Unused earned leave, up to 30 days, is paid out in the final settlement, which is processed within 45 days of the last working day.

## 7. Code of conduct

Employees must report conflicts of interest to HR in writing. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated and can be reported to the Internal Committee by email or through the anonymous form in the HR portal.
